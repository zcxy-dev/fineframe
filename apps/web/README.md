# Web page

One page in front of [`pnpm edit`](../../packages/engine/README.md#editing-footage): drop footage and music in or paste a video's link, choose the length, the aspect and a style, press start, watch the progress, then play the finished video and download it with its captions.

```bash
mise run start      # or pnpm start, with mise activated
```

`pnpm start` at the repository root ([`scripts/start.ts`](scripts/start.ts)) checks Node, pnpm and uv, says what to install when one is missing, warns when the disk has less than 10 GB free, runs `pnpm install` and `uv sync` in `worker/`, builds the page when its code or the packages it uses changed, starts the server and opens http://127.0.0.1:3000 in the browser (`pnpm start --no-open` leaves the browser alone). When the page already runs there, it only opens it; another program on the port is named as the problem. Ctrl+C stops the server. `mise run start` does the same where only mise is installed, installing the pinned Node, Python and uv first. `pnpm -C apps/web build` and `pnpm -C apps/web start` still build and start the page alone.

- **Address:** the server listens on 127.0.0.1 only. `VIDEO_EDITOR_HOST` and `PORT` change that, such as `VIDEO_EDITOR_HOST=100.x.y.z` for a private network; the page has no accounts, so never put it on a public address. Requests addressed to any other name are refused, so another site cannot reach the page by resolving its own name to this machine; `VIDEO_EDITOR_ALLOWED_HOSTS` adds names, comma-separated, for a proxy in front such as `tailscale serve`, without changing the address.
- **Desktop app:** the [desktop app](../desktop/README.md) runs this page in its own window, with its server on a free loopback port. There, the server keeps the keys in the app's own folder and installs the analysis's Python environment on its first start (`VIDEO_EDITOR_SETUP=1`), showing the download in the page; an edit cannot start until it is installed.
- **Inputs:** videos, still images and audio files, dropped as files or whole folders or chosen with a picker. Videos are cut and stills can show as B-roll. Beside the drop zone, "Paste a link" takes one or more links to public videos, mixed freely with files: YouTube's, and others yt-dlp reads, such as Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Vimeo and Bilibili, which may work. The server reads each link as it is pasted (`POST /api/links`), so the list shows its title and length and the cost estimate counts it, and refuses a playlist or channel, asking for one video's link, and a private, members-only, age-restricted or live video, saying so. A line beside the field says to import only videos you have the right to use. Audio files (by their extension, as `pnpm edit` sorts them) are music, passed with [`--music`](../../packages/engine/README.md#editing-footage): Claude picks the track that fits, or none, and the done page names the one the video plays. "Don't use" beside a track leaves it out. Music needs the licence it is used under, such as CC0 1.0, typed once for every track and passed as `--music-license`; use only music you have the right to use. The length is a preset (15 s, 30 s, 1 min, 3 min, 10 min) or typed, from 15 s to 1 h; the aspect is 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9; an optional box tells Claude what the owner wants of the edit, such as what to open with or leave out, in up to 2000 characters, passed as [`--instructions`](../../packages/engine/README.md#editing-footage); unlike the names and terms it starts empty for every edit, and the done page says the edit was planned with instructions; the style is one of the engine's [looks](../../packages/engine/README.md#colour) or none; the caption style (`--captions`) is one of the engine's [five](../../packages/engine/README.md#captions), each with a moving preview: Highlight (`bold`, the default), Bold (`creator`), Minimal, Pop or None, which puts no captions on the video and still makes the subtitle file; people's names (`--names`) and other terms (`--terms`), one per line, help the speech model and the correction spell them, only a name can show in a lower third, and both are remembered for the next edit (in `remembered.json` in the jobs' folder); advanced settings, collapsed, choose the quality (`--quality`) and transcript correction (`--correct`); visuals are rich, Claude's motion graphics written for the video and B-roll for each point (web pages and stock footage, beside the speaker or full frame, with soft sound effects), or minimal, none of it, passed as `--visuals`.
- **Several shorts:** "What to make" chooses one video, the default, or several shorts ([`--shorts`](../../packages/engine/README.md#editing-footage)). With several shorts, Length becomes the range each short's length lies in (15–30 s, 30–60 s, the default, 60–90 s or 90 s–3 min), and a count, 1 to 10, 5 unless chosen, appears; the aspect starts at 9:16. The estimate covers finding the moments and each short. The run is one project, listed as "5 shorts". While it runs, a grid shows each short as soon as it is made, in Claude's order, with places for those still being made; each card plays the short and gives its length, its stretch of the footage and its download. Once done, a short opens as an edit of its own, with its title, description and hashtags, each with a copy button, and the same versions, re-edit, Re-plan and caption style; a re-plan takes material only from its stretch and up to 30 s on each side, and plans it as a short about its moment again. Each short keeps the newest three versions' videos. Each short is saved into the project as it is made. When fewer shorts were made than asked, the page says how many of its moments could be made into a short of that length, or that the run stopped at its cost limit after them. A run cancelled or failed partway keeps the shorts it made, to play and download one by one or all together, and says they can no longer change. "Download all" gives one zip of every short's MP4 and SRT, named by its place and title (`01-<title>.mp4`, the title made safe as a file name on any system), and `shorts.txt` with each one's title, description and hashtags; the zip stores the files as they are, with UTF-8 names, and refuses past 4 GB or 65,535 files.
- **Speech:** heard on this computer, or on OpenRouter ([`--asr cloud`](../../worker/README.md#cloud-speech)) with the owner's OpenRouter key. A computer with too little memory for the local speech model ([how much](../../README.md#install)) starts on OpenRouter, and the page says why; either can be chosen.
- **Jobs:** each edit gets a folder in `~/.cache/video-editor/jobs/`, beside the engine's cache (`XDG_CACHE_HOME` moves both, `VIDEO_EDITOR_JOBS` only the jobs), outside the repository. Files stream into it, and when the edit starts the server fetches each link's video into it with the engine's [link import](../../packages/engine/README.md#editing-footage), named after its title, as footage like any other; the project list names a project after its first link's title; a name that could leave the folder, a hidden file, a file of another type, a file larger or smaller than announced, and footage that does not fit the free disk space are refused. The newest five finished projects keep their footage, so they can be edited again; creating an edit deletes the footage of older ones and of failed edits, and their outputs stay.
- **Projects:** the page lists every earlier edit, newest first, with the disk space its folder takes and whether its footage is kept; Open shows it again and Delete removes its folder. An edit under way cannot be deleted.
- **One edit at a time:** a second is refused while one runs, before its footage is sent, as one analysis already takes the machine's memory. Requests from another site are refused. Stopping the server stops a running edit.
- **Progress** comes from the command's JSON lines, sent to the page as Server-Sent Events: the stage, a bar that never goes back, the latest message, the analysis's steps for the file it is on (pending, running or done), and, once the analysis has run half a minute, an approximate time left: the analysis at the rate its bar has moved, then the plan, render and check from the edit's length ([`lib/eta.ts`](lib/eta.ts)), counting down without jumping. What an edit downloads on its first use shows its own bar with the bytes, the percentage, the speed and the time left at its rate so far: the analysis's models (the speech model, its aligner and the audio tagger; 3.5 GB in all on a CPU), FFmpeg, and Chrome Headless Shell and ffprobe for the effects. So does each link's video as it is fetched, before the analysis, and yt-dlp when it is new; Cancel stops the download. The job's id is in the page's address, so a reload or a bookmark opens it again; a running edit keeps going with the page closed.
- **Outputs:** the video plays in the page; MP4 and SRT downloads, and with B-roll, the credits of the stock footage, icons, screenshots and sound effects it shows (`<name>.broll.txt`).
- **Changing a finished edit:** below the player, the transcript of the video as edited: select any run of words (click one, Shift-click another) and delete it, or fix a caption word's spelling. Each edge of a deleted stretch moves to a pause a cut fits in inside the selection, as [`--cut`](../../packages/engine/README.md#changing-an-edit) does, and the page marks where it lands before anything renders; a selection with no such pause inside grows to the nearest ones outside it, and the page names the words that adds; the edit's planned length becomes its new one. The title's and lower thirds' text, the music's volume or its removal, the look, the caption style (with the same previews; a new style only renders again, with no new plan and no Claude cost) and each overlay, graphic or B-roll clip change there too. Changes pile up until Apply renders them once as a new version, while the one before keeps playing. Every version keeps its `project.json`; only the newest three, and the one shown, keep their video, and an older one renders again from its record when it is shown. Reordering, putting back what the plan left out, another length beyond ±5 %, another aspect or another music track need a new plan: Re-plan plans the edit again from the same footage, its analysis kept, at the length, aspect and music chosen there and with what the owner tells Claude ([`--instructions`](../../packages/engine/README.md#editing-footage)), keeping the shown version's look and caption style; Claude decides the story order and what to keep again. An edit made with 0.4.0 opens without its motion-graphics cards and keyword pops, which this version no longer draws, and the panel says that Re-plan gives it the new motion graphics. It becomes a new version with what the plan cost, and the downloads and summary follow the version shown; the summary gives the length asked for beside the one the video came out at. Keys work there too: Space plays or pauses, the arrow keys move between the transcript's words (one stop in the tab order) and with Shift select them, Delete or Backspace deletes the selection, Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on a Mac) undoes the last change not yet applied, and the arrow keys step the player one frame while it has focus; none of them act while typing in a field.
- **Keys:** with no Anthropic API key, the page asks for one when it opens, and "API key saved · Replace" in its header replaces it; with speech on OpenRouter and no OpenRouter key, it asks for that one. Free [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/api/) and [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/api/docs/) keys, for stock footage with rich visuals, are optional and added under the visuals setting; without them, visuals are motion graphics and web pages. The server checks a key at no cost, by looking up the planning model with Anthropic, reading the key's own details with OpenRouter, or searching once for a clip with Pexels or Pixabay, and saves only a key its service accepts, in the git-ignored `.env` at the repository root (`VIDEO_EDITOR_ENV_FILE` moves it; the desktop app keeps it in its own folder), written whole with permissions 0600 (on Windows, an access list that keeps out the machine's other users) and its other lines kept. A key goes to its service and to `pnpm edit`, and never back to the page or into a log. A key saved there is used over `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, `PEXELS_API_KEY` or `PIXABAY_API_KEY` in the server's environment, which counts when the file has none. An edit without the keys it needs is refused before its footage is sent.
- **Cost:** before an edit, the page estimates what Claude will cost from the footage's length, the edit's and whether it has rich visuals, fitted to the [measured edits](../../packages/engine/MEASUREMENTS.md#edit-measurements); after it, what planning, the motion graphics and the cut checks cost at list price, from the command's done line. A failed edit says what it cost before it stopped.
- **Failures** say in plain words what happened and what to do, with the program's own message behind "Show details" and every local path in it cut down to the file's name. Fixable ones name the step: no key or a key Anthropic or OpenRouter refuses (with a button to replace it), an account out of credit (with a link to add some), a full disk (also checked before the footage is sent), a file that cannot be read or cannot be found, and no connection. A failed edit whose footage is still in the page can be tried again as it was asked for.
